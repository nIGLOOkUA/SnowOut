# SnowOut

**SnowOut** is a 2D survival platformer with an action-packed vehicle finale.

## Story & Setting

Set high in the mountains during a fierce night storm, the game follows a climber whose camp is buried by a sudden, massive avalanche. You survive at the bottom of a deep canyon, cut off from your team. With your radio broken, your GPS shattered, and temperatures dropping fast, you are in serious danger.
To stay warm and call for help, you must climb up to an old weather station on the mountain ridge. Making your way past rubble, deep ice cracks, and abandoned tunnels, you search old camps for gear that lets you reach higher paths. Near the top pass, you find a heavy snow vehicle with a giant ice plow. You refuel it, smash through the garage gates, and speed up the mountain road—crashing through walls of ice to reach the station.

## Key Features

* **Atmospheric High-Altitude Survival:** Traverse treacherous night-time mountain terrain amid a raging winter blizzard. 
* **Exploration & Progression:** Explore abandoned mining workings and forgotten camps to recover climbing gear that unlocks new vertical paths.
* **Action-Packed Finale:** Take control of a heavy-duty snowcat with an icebreaker blade to plow through frozen blockades in the final push to the summit.

## Tech Stack

* **Engine:** Cocos2d-x 3.17.2
* **Language:** C++
* **Build System:** Windows, Android
