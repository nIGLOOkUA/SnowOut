# SnowOut

**SnowOut** is a 2D survival platformer with an action-packed vehicle finale.

## Story & Setting

Set in high-altitude mountains during a fierce nocturnal winter storm, the game follows a mountaineer whose base camp in the valley is buried by a sudden, massive avalanche.[cite: 2] Miraculously surviving at the bottom of a deep gorge and cut off from the rest of the expedition, the protagonist faces a grim reality: the camp's radio is destroyed, the navigator is shattered, and temperatures are plummeting.

To avoid fatal hypothermia and call for rescue, the climber must ascend to an old weather station high on the mountain ridge. Navigating through debris, icy crevasses, and abandoned mountain tunnels, the hero scavenges gear from old campsites to unlock new upward routes.[cite: 2] Near the final mountain pass, the climber discovers an all-terrain snowcat equipped with a massive ice-breaking plow—refueling the rig, smashing through the hangar gates, and barreling up the summit road through walls of ice to reach the station.

## Key Features

* **Atmospheric High-Altitude Survival:** Traverse treacherous night-time mountain terrain amid a raging winter blizzard.[cite: 2]
* **Exploration & Progression:** Explore abandoned mining workings and forgotten camps to recover climbing gear that unlocks new vertical paths.
* **Action-Packed Finale:** Take control of a heavy-duty snowcat with an icebreaker blade to plow through frozen blockades in the final push to the summit.
